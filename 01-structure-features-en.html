2-Tier LLM

The Supervisor routes text and app tasks. AppConcierge focuses on app selection and parameter extraction.

Personalized Routing

Combine the installed-app inventory with a curated catalog. Only available apps become candidates, with dynamic system-prompt context at login.

Lifecycle Tracking

Track requested → opened → returned with a handoff_id. Failures and return events feed directly into the LLM context.
